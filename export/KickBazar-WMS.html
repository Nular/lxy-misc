Outbound Orders

Import Admin-exported orders. One import creates one Outbound No. Stock is occupied on allocate and deducted only after the courier pickup (Confirm Shipment).

1 Import orders 2 Allocate or On Hold 3 Export → fill Tracking No. → import 4 Print pick list 5 Pick → Pack 6 Confirm Shipment
The selected warehouse applies only to this import. Switching context later does not change existing outbound records.
